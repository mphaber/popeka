How to play

Tap a group of two or more bubbles of the same color and the whole group pops.

Bubbles above fall down to fill the gaps, and empty columns slide over to close up.

Clear the whole board to finish the level. If only single bubbles are left, they pop on their own in a sparkly finish, so you can never get stuck.

Rainbow bubbles match every color at once.

Boom bubbles (with the spark badge) pop everything touching the group when their group pops.

Ice bubbles do not pop until two pops next to them melt the ice away.

Finish a level to unlock the next one. Finished levels can be replayed any time.

Each level gives you a tap budget. Run out of taps and you can simply try the level again.

Finish in fewer taps to earn more stars.
